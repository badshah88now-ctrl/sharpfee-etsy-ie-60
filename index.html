Etsy Fees in Ireland: What a €60 Order Really Costs

Irish sellers on Etsy pay the standard EU fee stack, and on a €60 order it adds up to more than most sellers expect. Four separate charges apply, and each one is small enough to miss — together they take about €6.80 of every €60 sale.

The listing fee is $0.20 per listing, billed in US dollars, roughly €0.20. The transaction fee is 6.5% of the total price including shipping — €3.90 on €60. Payment processing for EU sellers is 4% plus €0.30, another €2.70. Total: about €6.80, leaving around €53.20 before the cost of the product, packaging, and postage.

That is before any optional extras. Etsy's Offsite Ads can take a further 12% or 15% of the sale if an ad brought the buyer, and promoted listings add their own cost per click. A seller who turns on every promotion without checking the maths can watch a €60 sale shrink fast. So when does your own store get cheaper? Shopify's Basic plan in Ireland costs €32 a month, and each order costs 2% plus €0.25 through Shopify Payments — €1.45 on a €60 order. That is €5.35 less per order than Etsy's €6.80. Divide the €32 monthly fee by that €5.35 saving and the crossover sits at roughly six orders a month. Sell more than six €60 orders a month and your own store starts winning on fees alone, before you count the control over branding, customer data, and repeat buyers.

The honest answer is not "Etsy is expensive" or "Shopify is always better". It depends on volume. Under six orders a month, Etsy's per-order fees are cheaper than paying a monthly subscription. Above that, the subscription pays for itself.
